Garage Door FAQ for Michigan Property Owners

How quickly can you reach Southfield and nearby cities?

We prioritize urgent issues and serve a 35-mile radius from Southfield. Exact arrival time depends on traffic, weather, and service queue, but emergency cases get first priority.

Do you handle both residential and commercial garage doors?

Yes. We provide repair, maintenance, and installation services for homes, warehouses, and commercial facilities across Metro Detroit.

Should I try replacing a broken spring myself?

No. Garage door springs are under extreme tension and can cause severe injury. Spring replacement should always be handled by trained technicians.

Can you install insulated doors for Michigan winters?

Absolutely. We recommend insulated, weather-sealed models to improve comfort and reduce energy loss in attached garages.
