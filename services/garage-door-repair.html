Garage Door Repair in Southfield, MI and Metro Detroit

If your garage door is noisy, slow, misaligned, or not responding, our technicians diagnose the root issue and complete repairs with safety checks, hardware adjustments, and performance testing.

Common Repair Calls We Handle

  • Off-track garage doors caused by impact, wear, or cable issues.
  • Broken rollers and hinges creating loud vibration and door drag.
  • Damaged cables affecting balance and lift performance.
  • Bent panels or alignment problems reducing door seal and security.
  • Safety sensor faults causing reverse or non-closing behavior.
