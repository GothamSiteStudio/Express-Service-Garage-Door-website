Garage Door Maintenance and Tune-Up Service in Southfield, MI

Scheduled maintenance keeps your garage door quiet, safe, and reliable while helping prevent sudden breakdowns during Michigan winter and high-use periods.

What Is Included in a Tune-Up

  • Spring and cable inspection for wear and tension consistency.
  • Track and roller review for alignment and smooth travel.
  • Lubrication service for hinges, bearings, and moving components.
  • Sensor and auto-reverse tests for operational safety.
  • Opener setting checks for force, travel, and response balance.
