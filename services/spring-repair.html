Broken Garage Door Spring Repair in Southfield, MI

We replace worn or snapped torsion and extension springs with the right cycle rating for your door weight and usage pattern. Proper spring sizing protects your opener and restores safe balance.

Warning Signs of Spring Failure

  • Loud bang in garage often indicates spring breakage.
  • Door feels extremely heavy when attempting manual lift.
  • Door opens unevenly or lifts only a few inches.
  • Visible spring gap in torsion system above the door.

Do not attempt DIY spring replacement. Springs are under high tension and can cause severe injury.

How long does spring replacement take?

Most jobs are completed in a single visit after system inspection.

Should both springs be replaced together?

Usually yes. Replacing both helps maintain balanced performance and reliability.

Can I open the door with a broken spring?

It is not recommended. Forced operation can damage the opener and create safety risks.
